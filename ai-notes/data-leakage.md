# Data Leakage

Data leakage happens when information that should not be available during training accidentally influences the model.

This can lead to unrealistically high performance during testing.

Preventing data leakage is important for building reliable AI systems.

Data leakage occurs when information from outside the training data is accidentally used to train a model.

This causes unrealistically high performance during evaluation.

Prevent data leakage by carefully separating training and testing data.
